# Issue Questionnaire

Fill in as much as you can, in your own words — plain speech beats polished answers.
Where a question doesn't apply, delete it. Long answers welcome; the editors cut,
they don't pad. Anything you say may be quoted verbatim in the issue.

## The story

1. **In one or two sentences: what do you actually do, day to day?**
   (Not the brochure version — the real work.)

2. **How did you get here? Walk through it year by year where you can.**
   First job, the turn, who said what, when you decided. Dates matter.

3. **What is the single sentence you live by, or say most often?**
   (This often becomes the cover line or a pull quote.)

4. **What do people misunderstand about your work?**

## The record

5. **Credentials, awards, recognitions — with years and places.**
   Institution names exactly as they should appear.

6. **Press or media coverage worth naming.**

7. **Numbers you're willing to print.** Portfolio size, years in the field,
   clients served, deals closed — only what's true and citable.

## The texture

8. **Tell one client (or project) story in full.**
   Who came to you, what they wanted, what went wrong or right, how it ended.
   The more specific, the better — this becomes a scene in the issue.

9. **What is the unglamorous part of the job that actually makes it work?**

10. **Describe the place you work from — the city, the island, the street.**
    What does it smell/look/feel like? Why does the place matter to the work?

## Words in your voice

11. **Three to five phrases you actually say** — to clients, to your team,
    to yourself. Verbatim.

12. **A piece of advice for someone who wants to do what you do.**
    Honest, not inspirational.

## Ahead

13. **Where is this going in the next five years?** Concrete ambitions.

14. **What would you never do, no matter the money?**
